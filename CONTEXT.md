# Fitness Insights Domain Glossary

## Owner
The single person whose nutrition and training history is analyzed: Trevis.

## Source export
A file exported from a tracking application and supplied by the owner for analysis.

## Import
The act of bringing source-export records into the project. The owner performs imports weekly through the app.

## Dashboard
The exploratory view of nutrition, weight, activity, and exercise metrics with supporting charts.

## Written summary
A narrative assembled from reproducible calculations and predefined wording, accompanying the dashboard.

## AI analysis
Open-ended interpretation using an AI model. An eventual interest, outside the initial calculation-based experience.

## Completed day
A past local calendar day eligible for completed-period analysis. Today and future dates are excluded; owner-marked incomplete days are excluded from affected metrics.

## Suspicious value
A recorded value awaiting owner review because it triggered a validation rule. It remains preserved in the source record and is temporarily omitted from dependent calculations until kept or excluded.

## Comparable sessions
Exercise sessions whose movement variant, equipment, and recording conventions permit a meaningful performance comparison.

## Import version
A recoverable state associated with a confirmed import. Recent versions support undo and report comparison without limiting the total history retained.

## Source estimate
A value estimated by the tracking application, such as expenditure or trend weight, rather than calculated by this project.

## Logged load
The resistance recorded by the owner: per hand for dumbbells, with no automatic doubling and no inferred machine or body weight. For hack squat and glute ham raises, only the recorded weight counts.

## Logged repetitions
Repetitions recorded per side for unilateral movements; not automatically doubled.

## Source domain
A category of imported history such as daily measurements, food entries, or workout sets. Replacing one domain's records does not erase other domains on the same date.

## Import coverage
Dates explicitly represented for a source domain. A missing date alone does not mean its previously stored records were deleted.
